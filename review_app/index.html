How to review a tile (≈1–2 min each)

You see a 640 × 640 px piece of a slide with the model's outlines. Your job is only to fix what is wrong — correct outlines need no action.

1 · Wrong outline

  • Click the outline → choose the right class (viable / non-viable / intermediate) or “Not a pollen grain” (debris, bubble, stain blob).
  • One outline covering two touching grains: keep it, and add the second grain as missed.

2 · Missed grain

  • Click on the grain where there is no outline → choose its class. A ⊕ marker appears.
  • Click a ⊕ marker again to remove it.
  • Ignore grains cut by the tile border if less than half is visible.

3 · Verdict & save

  • Good: no or trivial errors · Minor: a few errors · Poor: systematic problem.
  • Tick Image problem if the image itself is bad. Then Save & next.

Classes (Alexander stain)

  • Viable: full, dark magenta/purple.
  • Non-viable: empty, pale green/blue, shrivelled.
  • Intermediate: partly stained / mottled.

Keyboard

M outlines on/off · hold Space raw image · A/S/D verdict · Enter save & next · ←/→ navigate · in a menu: 1 viable, 2 non-viable, 3 intermediate, X not pollen, C correct, Esc close.
