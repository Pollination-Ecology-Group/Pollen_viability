## 1. Short version for the manuscript

**Automated pollen viability and 3D morphometric phenotyping.** Pollen production, gametophyte viability, and three-dimensional (3D) grain size were quantified across 1,008 whole-slide brightfield scans (two sister slides per flower across 504 flowering plants out of 540 cultivated individuals — 36 plants never flowered — spanning four cytotypes: `ory_2x`, `nat_2x`, `nat_4x`, and `neo_4x` across five *Arabidopsis* populations: `AA012`, `AA025`, `AA225`, `AA310`, and `AA372`, and two F2 cohorts: `run1_F2` and `run3_F2`). Mature anthers at anthesis were stained using Alexander's differential protocol (Alexander, 1969), distinguishing cytoplasm-filled **viable** pollen (dark magenta), empty **non-viable** microspores (turquoise/green cell walls), and partially stained **intermediate** grains. Whole coverslips were scanned on an automated Zeiss Axio Scan.Z1 microscope at $0.8772\,\mu\text{m px}^{-1}$ and analyzed in overlapping $640 \times 640\text{ px}$ tiles ($64\text{ px}$ overlap; 265,520 tiles total) using a three-class `YOLOv11m-seg` instance-segmentation model (Ultralytics; Jocher et al., 2023) trained over three active-learning rounds on 773 tiles (7,427 verified grain outlines plus 11.5% background/debris tiles) annotated with Segment Anything (`SAM`; Kirillov et al., 2023). Full-resolution grain outlines were smoothed without altering grain area, and detections straddling overlapping tile borders were merged ($\text{IoU} > 0.35$) to prevent double counting.

**Debris & focus filtering, 3D grain size reconstruction, validation, and statistical analysis.** To remove tiny stain precipitates without discarding naturally small shrunken aborted grains, minimum diameter and confidence thresholds were calibrated separately for each cytotype and viability class (2,186,245 calibrated pollen grains across the 1,008 scans). Because local optical Z-defocus blurs dark viable pollen into pale brownish halos that inflate apparent grain diameter and mimic non-viable staining, each grain's contrast-normalized Laplacian sharpness ($100 \times \text{SD}(|\nabla^2 I|) / \Delta I$) was evaluated across all slides: locally out-of-focus grains ($< 3.50$; 260,729 detections, 11.9%) were excluded before computing viability and grain size (1,925,516 in-focus grains), while retaining total calibrated counts per slide for pollen abundance. At the slide level, 22 low-count pipetting-failure scans ($<100$ total grains; losing 3 plants with $<100$ grains on both sister slides) and 9 wholly out-of-focus scans ($<100$ sharp grains; losing 1 plant) were excluded, retaining **977 whole-slide scans across 500 of the 504 flowering plants** (477 plants with both sister slides and 23 with one passing sister slide; 2,179,117 calibrated and 1,924,830 in-focus grains). Grain size was measured on 1,679,037 in-focus isolated single grains after excluding touching clumps and outer scan-border grains ($\text{solidity} \ge 0.88$, $\text{aspect ratio} \le 1.35$). To account for preferential gravitational settling of non-spherical grains onto microscope slides, we reconstructed 3D grain shape (Erdtman, 1952), 3D grain volume ($V_{3\text{D}}$), and 3D volume-equivalent spherical diameter ($D_{3\text{D}}$) per cytotype from the joint distribution of 2D grain diameters, ellipse aspect ratios, and polar vs. equatorial grain silhouettes (full stereological and 3D mesh equations in Supplementary Methods). Pipeline accuracy was evaluated blindly by two independent observers across 154 unseen tiles (100 tiles per observer, including 46 shared double-blind tiles; 200 tile-reviews and 2,595 evaluated grains, plus a 96-grain audit of the $\le \text{P2}$ and $\ge \text{P95}$ size tails). On the 46 shared tiles, inter-observer agreement reached $r = 0.999$ for grain count and $r = 0.947$ for viability (median $|\Delta| = 0.00\text{ pp}$, mean $|\Delta| = 1.77\text{ pp}$). Across all 200 blind reviews, the calibrated pipeline achieved **98.0% precision**, **99.0% recall**, **99.3% three-class accuracy**, and **97.5% single-grain outline accuracy** ($+1.48\text{ pp}$ mean viability bias, smaller than inter-observer human variation and verified to have no effect on mixed-model conclusions). Traits were analyzed with linear mixed-effects models (`lme4` / `lmerTest`; Bates et al., 2015) with fixed effect `cytotype`, random intercepts for population, cultivation cohort, and plant identity, variance partitioning ($R^2_m, R^2_c$; Nakagawa & Schielzeth, 2013), and Tukey-adjusted pairwise comparisons (`emmeans`; Piepho, 2004).

## 3. Long version (Detailed Materials & Methods / Supplementary Methods)

### 3.1 Sample preparation, whole-slide scanning, and experimental design

Out of 540 cultivated *Arabidopsis* individuals (`run1_F2` = 180, `run3_F2` = 360) spanning five geographic populations (`AA012`, `AA025`, `AA225`, `AA310`, `AA372`), 504 plants flowered at sampling (`run1_F2` = 176, `run3_F2` = 328; 36 plants never flowered) across four cytotypes: natural diploids (`nat_2x`, $2n = 2x$), oryzalin-treated diploid controls that remained diploid (`ory_2x`, $2n = 2x$), established natural autotetraploids (`nat_4x`, $2n = 4x$), and newly synthesized autotetraploids (`neo_4x`, $2n = 4x$). Anthers at anthesis were stained in modified Alexander's differential stain (Alexander, 1969), in which Acid Fuchsin stains intact protoplasm of viable gametes dark magenta (`class 0: viable`), Malachite Green stains cellulose and sporopollenin walls of aborted cytoplasm-empty microspores turquoise/blue-green (`class 1: non_viable`), and partially vacuolated grains stain mottled pale pink (`class 2: intermediate`). Each floral pollen suspension was split into two aliquots mounted under separate coverslips (`primary` and `replicate` sister slides; 1,008 whole-slide scans total) to control for pipetting and coverslip variability. Whole slides were digitized under brightfield illumination on an automated Zeiss Axio Scan.Z1 scanner at $0.877242\,\mu\text{m px}^{-1}$ ($0.769554\,\mu\text{m}^2\text{ px}^{-2}$) and partitioned into 265,520 coordinate-registered $640 \times 640\text{ px}$ JPEG tiles ($\text{stride} = 576\text{ px}$, $64\text{ px}$ overlap).

### 3.2 Three-class instance segmentation and active-learning training (`v1.0`–`v3.1`)

Pollen grains were segmented and classified simultaneously using a three-class `YOLOv11m-seg` architecture (Ultralytics 8.4; Jocher et al., 2023) initialized from COCO pretrained weights (Lin et al., 2014). Across three active-learning rounds (`v1.0` $\rightarrow$ `v3.1`), hard false positives (stain precipitates, anther wall debris, coverslip edges, air-bubble rims) and rare `nat_4x`/`neo_4x` morphologies were curated in a web-based Segment Anything (`SAM`; Kirillov et al., 2023) Polygon Labeling Studio (`viability-label-stenc.dyn.cloud.e-infra.cz`). Training-tile sharpness was gated strictly on **grain-local Laplacian variance** ($\text{Laplacian}_{\text{grain}} \ge 35.0$; Pech-Pacheco et al., 2000), excluding empty slide-border `_tile_0_` margins and disabling synthetic $4\times$ zoom augmentation on tetraploid slides, while holding out all 870 training/relabel tiles from blind validation. The final network (`pollen_train_20261122_1041`) was trained on 773 tiles (7,427 polygon masks: 62.1% `viable`, 28.9% `non_viable`, 9.1% `intermediate`, plus 11.5% pure hard-negative background tiles) for 200 epochs in full FP32/TF32 precision (`amp = False`, AdamW, `lr0 = 0.0008`, cosine decay, 0/200 NaN epochs) with mosaic deactivated for the final 25 epochs (`close_mosaic = 25`); the post-mosaic full-scale checkpoint (`last_20261122_r3.pt`, MD5 `1ea93497105eee085848310df127e42f`) halved false positives on hard QC tiles and reduced slide-level viability bias threefold.

### 3.3 Cluster inference, cross-tile stitching, two-tier calibration, and optical focus filtering

Whole-dataset inference across all 265,520 tiles ran as a 6-shard Indexed GPU Job on CERIT-SC (`CONF = 0.50`, class-agnostic NMS `IOU = 0.45`, containment suppression $> 0.65$, maximum box side $130\text{ px}$). High-resolution masks (`retina_masks = True`) underwent $2\times$ supersampled morphological opening and area-preserving Chaikin corner-cutting, and border-cut predictions within $6\text{ px}$ of internal tile seams were merged in global slide coordinates ($\text{IoU} > 0.35$, paginated across all 1,008 slide prefixes). Empirical per-`(cytotype, class)` minimum diameter (`class_min_diam.csv`, retaining $>99\%$ of isolated reference grains) and confidence (`class_conf_threshold.csv`) thresholds suppressed fine dye precipitates without censoring shrunken sterile microspores (2,186,245 calibrated grains across 1,008 whole-slide scans). To prevent local Z-defocus halos from washing out magenta viable stain or inflating grain contours, contrast-normalized Laplacian sharpness ($100 \times \text{SD}(|\nabla^2 I|) / \max(18, P_{95} - P_{05}) \ge 3.50$) was computed around each grain across all 1,008 slides (`grain_focus_flags.csv.gz`), filtering out 260,729 (11.9%) locally out-of-focus detections and retaining 1,925,516 in-focus grains overall (`1,924,830` across **977 QC-passing whole-slide scans from 500 of the 504 flowering plants** after excluding 22 sparse scans with $<100$ total grains [losing 3 plants] and 9 wholly defocused scans with $<100$ sharp grains [losing 1 plant]; halving sister-slide viability discordance from $6.24\text{ pp}$ to $3.20\text{ pp}$, median $1.01\text{ pp}$). Single-grain 2D area $A_{2\text{D}}$, equivalent diameter $D_{2\text{D}} = 2\sqrt{A_{2\text{D}}/\pi}$, and fitted ellipse axes were extracted on all 1,679,037 in-focus isolated non-border `viable`/`non_viable` grains (`is_outer_edge == 0`, `solidity >= 0.88`, `aspect_ratio <= 1.35`).

### 3.4 Two-stage stereological 3D unfolding and closed surface-mesh reconstruction

Because non-spherical pollen grains settle preferentially onto their broader face under viscous/gravitational settling on microscope slides, true 3D dimensions and volumes were reconstructed per cytotype following the *Pollen Latitude* 3D reconstruction methodology (`reconstruct_3d_species.py` / `reconstruct_3d_cytotypes.py`):

1. **Stereological 3D axis and settling unfolding:** For all 1,571,294 in-focus hydrated isolated viable grains (529,299 `nat_2x`, 280,294 `ory_2x`, 333,330 `nat_4x`, 428,371 `neo_4x`), slide-normalized log equivalent diameter $u = \ln(D_{2\text{D}} / D_{\text{slide,med}})$ and half-log ellipse aspect ratio $\delta = \frac{1}{2}\ln(a'/b')$ were compared against quasi-Monte Carlo projections ($M = 600$ Fibonacci hemisphere orientations) of four nested 3D ellipsoid families (`sphere`, `prolate`, `oblate`, `triaxial` with canonical semi-axes $1 \ge p \ge q$) weighted by Boltzmann slide-settling strength $\tau \in [0, 3.5]$, biological log-size dispersion $\sigma_g$, and 2D contour axis noise $\sigma_e$. Parameters were estimated via differential evolution minimizing 24-angle 2D Sliced Wasserstein Distance (requiring $\ge 8\%$ relative SWD improvement over simpler nested models, with 32 slide-block bootstrap refits for standard errors), yielding homologous `prolate-spheroidal` Erdtman (1952) shapes across all four cytotypes ($\tau = 0.73\text{–}0.75$) and stereological volume factors $k_{\text{ell,3D}} = (p\,q)/d_{\text{sim,med}}^3$.
2. **Non-ellipsoidal closed genus-0 surface mesh:** Up to 160 polar ($\le Q_{25}$ aspect ratio, phase-aligned on the dominant 3-fold tricolpate Fourier harmonic) and 160 equatorial ($\ge Q_{75}$ aspect ratio) 2D grain contours per cytotype were sampled from S3 `_det.json` files to build closed triangulated Wavefront `.obj` meshes (`pollen_3d_meshes.zip`). Exact divergence-theorem integration relative to a matched reference ellipsoid grid gave the non-ellipsoidal lobation factor $\phi_{\text{mesh}}$ (`1.0013–1.0030`), slide-level median 3D grain volume $V_{3\text{D,med}} = \frac{\pi}{6} D_{2\text{D,med}}^3 k_{\text{ell,3D}} \phi_{\text{mesh}}$, orientation-deconvolved mean 3D grain volume $V_{3\text{D,mean}}$, and 3D volume-equivalent spherical diameter $D_{3\text{D}} = (6 V_{3\text{D,med}}/\pi)^{1/3}$.

### 3.5 Two-reviewer blinded human quality control, archiving, and references

Across $154$ unseen sharpness-verified interior tiles ($200$ blind tile-reviews: $100$ per observer across two independent reviewers with $46$ shared double-blind overlap tiles; $2,595$ evaluated detections; $0$ image-issue rejections; `r3_final_20261123_1702`) in the blinded web portal (`viability-review-stenc.dyn.cloud.e-infra.cz`), inter-rater agreement on the $46$ shared double-blind tiles reached **$r = 0.999$ for grain count** ($5.7\%$ mean relative difference) and **$r = 0.947$ for viability** ($\text{median } |\Delta| = 0.00\text{ pp}$, $\text{mean } |\Delta| = 1.77\text{ pp}$; $88.1\%$ of tiles within $\pm 2\text{ pp}$). Pooled across both reviewers, raw predictions achieved **$95.50\%$ end-to-end accuracy** ($97.19\%$ precision, $99.17\%$ recall, $99.08\%$ class accuracy, $97.50\%$ polygon accuracy; $+1.13\text{ pp}$ raw viability bias — smaller than the $1.77\text{ pp}$ inter-observer human difference), rising after calibration to **$98.0\%$ precision**, **$99.0\%$ recall**, **$99.3\%$ class accuracy** ($\sim 96.3\%$ pooled end-to-end; $+1.48\text{ pp}$ calibrated viability bias). Propagating either pooled two-reviewer calibrated adjustments or Reviewer 2's stricter uncalibrated deviations alone left every mixed-model $F$-test significance and Tukey post-hoc grouping unchanged, and a $96$-grain blind audit of $\le \text{P2}$ and $\ge \text{P95}$ size tails (`extremes_r3_20261124_1307`) confirmed genuine gamete morphology across tails. All tiles, weights, annotations, 3D `.obj` meshes, and CSV exports are archived in CESNET S3 (`s3://peg-bucket/Ostatni/Pollen_viability/`).
